Four numbers for every reaction

All of them are evaluated at any temperature you choose, using heat capacities of the form CP°/R = A + BT + CT² + DT⁻² from Smith, Van Ness & Abbott, Appendix C.

ΔH°

Heat of reaction. Negative means heat is released (exothermic).

ΔS°

Entropy change, which grows in weight as temperature rises.

ΔG°

Gibbs energy change. Below zero, products are favoured at equilibrium.

K

Equilibrium constant, plus the conversion you can expect at a chosen pressure.

How the results are checked

Balanced reactions only. Every reaction, including ones you type, is checked atom by atom before anything is calculated.

Textbook data. 34 species with ΔH°f, ΔG°f, S° and Cp coefficients, each row listing its source, reference state and valid temperature range.

Honest warnings. The solver says when a Cp correlation is stretched past its range or a liquid is pushed above its boiling point.

Tested against known answers. Worked textbook examples and published equilibrium constants (Moe 1962; Graaf et al. 1986) are reproduced in the error challenge.
